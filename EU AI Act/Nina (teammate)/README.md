# EU AI Act Peer Audit – Janaina Hoffmann's Project

This repository contains my independent first-pass EU AI Act compliance assessment of **Janaina Hoffmann's Project 3 – Company Research Bot**, completed as part of the AI Consulting Bootcamp.

## Repository structure

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Peer_Audit_Company_Research_Bot_EU_AI_Act.md` | Independent peer audit covering all required phases of the lab, including risk classification, clarification log, role mapping, compliance findings and recommendation. |
| `SYSTEM_BRIEF.md` | System brief provided by the project author and used as the basis for this independent assessment. |

## Scope

This assessment is based solely on the documentation provided by the project author and reflects an independent first-pass compliance review. It is **not** a legal opinion, conformity assessment or certification under the EU AI Act.
